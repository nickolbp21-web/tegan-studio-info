TEGAN STUDIO

Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026. Tegan Studio is a private application operated for its owner's social accounts.

Data accessed and used

With authorization, the app reads the connected YouTube channel's identity, profile images, public statistics, and video metadata. The app uses upload permission to publish videos and captions selected by the owner and to verify their processing and visibility. Similar authorized profile and publishing data is used for connected Meta accounts.

Storage and sharing

Video files, draft captions, account metadata, and publishing records are stored on the owner's computer. Remembered credentials are encrypted using a key protected by the owner's Windows account. Selected video files, captions, and publishing settings are sent to the selected social platform when publication is confirmed. Profile images and metadata are retrieved from the platforms to display account previews.

Caption assistance runs locally. Google user data is not used to train AI models. The application does not sell user data, provide it to advertisers, or send it to a cloud AI service. Data is used only to provide the features described here, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

The owner controls local storage and can remove local files and records. Disconnecting an account removes the app's saved credentials for that connection. Previously published social posts remain on the destination platform until the owner manages them there. Google access can also be revoked through Google Account connections.

This information site

This site contains application information only. It has no analytics scripts, sign-in form, or video uploads. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under its own policies.

Contact

The operator is the owner of the nickolbp21-web GitHub account. Contact the operator through that account for application or privacy questions.
